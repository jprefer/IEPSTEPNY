Mark your calendars for the 21st Annual International Estate Planning Institute, hosted by STEP New York. Join us on March 26 & 27, 2026, at the Marriott Marquis in Times Square for a dynamic one-and-a-half-day event.

What to Expect

The Institute will feature industry leaders who will present the latest topics and trends in estate and tax planning. Institute Registrants and STEP Members are also invited to a Pre-Institute Cocktail event on Wednesday, March 25th.

STEP Membership

STEP is a global professional body of over 22,000 legal, accounting, and trustee professionals dedicated to helping families plan for their futures. We uphold high professional standards, inform public policy, promote education, and connect practitioners worldwide.

STEP NY is the local branch of the Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners ("STEP"), a global professional organization of private client practitioners. Our primary purpose is to improve public understanding of the issues families face in relation to inheritance and succession planning, and to promote education and high professional standards among our members.
